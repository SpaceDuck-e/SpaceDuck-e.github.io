1984

That's right, I read the 1984 book!

Pretty good book in my opinion, but I thought that there was gonna be more to the ending. Ending was kinda mid in my opinion.

There might be a couple spoilers ahead, so beware.

If there was something I would change in the story is not letting it end in little Winston drinking very sad. Kind of anticlimatic when a couple pages earlier the book talked about the proles rissing and staying firm against their indoctrination and stuff.

But I also think that letting it end that way was way more deep. Remembering people that if no one thinks, you alone will not be able to change it. People need to actually think to achieve change.

Also, am planning on reading Farenheit 451 next, I remember having seen it in a Ted ed video along with 1984 and Animal Farm, both of which I have already read.
